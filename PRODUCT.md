# Product Context

## Purpose
A client-facing portfolio webpage showcasing systems engineering expertise, grounded in institutional state university development at the **Office of the College Secretary (OCS)** at the **University of the Philippines Los Baños (UPLB) College of Forestry and Natural Resources (CFNR)**, balanced with independent commercial client products.

## Identity & Role
- **Institution**: UPLB College of Forestry and Natural Resources (CFNR) — Office of the College Secretary (OCS).
- **Primary Domain**: High-stakes academic administrative operations, registrar workflows, student academic records, document tracking, graduation audits, prerequisite waivers, and digital clearance pipelines.
- **Commercial Track**: High-converting client web applications, custom databases, booking platforms, and workflow automation for private businesses.

## Key Projects Highlighted
1. **OCS Document Tracking & Verification Platform**: End-to-end tracking for academic petitions, true copy of grades (TCG), leave of absence (LOA), with QR verification and audit trails.
2. **Curricular Audit & Graduation Evaluation Engine**: Automated scholastic standing checks, prerequisite chains, and student clearance verification.
3. **Student Prerequisite Waiver & Enrollment Endorsement Core**: Multi-tier digital endorsement pipeline (Adviser -> Dept Chair -> College Secretary).
4. **Atlas Trucking & Multi-Hub Freight Operations Platform (Atlas Transport Corporation)**: Turborepo monorepo with Next.js 16 web consoles (Admin, Manager, Checker), React Native/Expo mobile scanner app, cryptographic SHA-256 seal locking, and Firestore real-time tracking across 1,222 commits.
5. **Commercial Client Booking & Intake Scheduling Platform**.
6. **Commercial Cloud POS & Real-Time Kitchen Display**.
