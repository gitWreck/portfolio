Visual Proof: Animated GIFs & Interaction Walkthroughs

How prospective clients evaluate live software workflows.

Should you use animated GIFs in your portfolio?

Yes, absolutely. A 6–10 second looping GIF showing mouse navigation, button clicks, and immediate UI feedback is the single most persuasive proof of competence. It shows prospective clients that your systems are real, functional, and intuitive to operate.

Best practices for recording project GIFs

Keep the camera steady—avoid aggressive zooming. Instead, capture a single clear user story: for example, searching an academic record → clicking Generate QR Certificate → seeing the verified output. Save as .gif or lightweight .webp under assets/screenshots/.
