# Product Context

## Purpose
A client-facing portfolio webpage showcasing systems engineering expertise, grounded in institutional state university development at the **Office of the College Secretary (OCS)** at the **University of the Philippines Los Baños (UPLB) College of Forestry and Natural Resources (CFNR)**, balanced with independent commercial client products.

## Identity & Role
- **Institution**: UPLB College of Forestry and Natural Resources (CFNR) — Office of the College Secretary (OCS).
- **Primary Domain**: High-stakes academic administrative operations, registrar workflows, student academic records, document tracking, graduation audits, prerequisite waivers, and digital clearance pipelines.
- **Commercial Track**: High-converting client web applications, custom databases, booking platforms, and workflow automation for private businesses.

## Key Projects Highlighted
1. **DTOCS-CFNR: Document Tracker & Kiosk Management System**: Comprehensive web-based document routing, touchscreen student kiosk, CSAR database integration, and real-time Node.js WebSocket queue display broadcasting for UPLB CFNR.
2. **Essentials Hub: Student Welfare Pantry & Resource Management Platform**: Self-service student welfare kiosk with daily quota bounds, CSAR verification, 5-second auto-reset timer, non-destructive inventory logs, and welfare demand analytics.
3. **Atlas Trucking & Multi-Hub Freight Operations Platform (Atlas Transport Corporation)**: Turborepo monorepo with Next.js 16 web consoles (Admin, Manager, Checker), React Native/Expo mobile scanner app, cryptographic SHA-256 seal locking, and Firestore real-time tracking across 1,222 commits.
4. **VOCO Operations Floor & Dispatch Workstation (VOCO Global Solutions)**: Enterprise caller workstation with live call timers, distraction-free caller mode, Cmd+K search, multi-tier ticket dispatch, and live team floor telemetry backed by PostgreSQL connection pooling.
5. **VOCO Client Acquisition Portal & Staffing Platform (VOCO Global Solutions)**: Accessible single-page virtual staffing portal with Next.js 16 Server Actions, parameterized SQL intake, and WCAG AA compliance (37/40 Impeccable heuristic score).
